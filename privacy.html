QuantWatch privacy policy

QuantWatch is used only by its owner. It requests the Google Drive "drive.file" permission, which lets it see and manage only files it creates in the owner's own Google Drive.

No Google user data is shared with, sold to, or sent to anyone else. Data stays in the owner's Google Drive and the owner's private GitHub repository.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
